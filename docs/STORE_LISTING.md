## Description
ViralLens helps creators review vertical shorts with multimodal AI analysis.

Upload an MP4, MOV, or WebM (up to 90 seconds, 100 MB). ViralLens returns:
- Overall and category scores (hook, pacing, storytelling, visuals, audio, CTA)
- Directional engagement ranges (not point predictions)
- Timestamped improvement suggestions
- Hook, caption, hashtag, CTA, and shot-plan revision ideas

You always see how many analyses remain. Plans never claim unlimited analysis.

**Plans**
- Intro trial: up to 3 completed analyses
- Monthly Pro: 30 analyses per billing period
- Annual Pro: 40 analyses per month

Raw videos are stored privately, deleted after analysis (or failure), and expire within 24 hours. Delete individual analyses or wipe your account anytime in Settings.

**Disclaimer:** Estimates are directional and do not guarantee social-media performance.
